**Evidence stages** (use exactly one per row):

- **Demo:** shown by the vendor or a lab, at an event, in a video, or at a staged site. No customer workflow.
- **Customer pilot:** runs at a customer site on the customer's task, usually time-limited and possibly unpaid.
- **Paid deployment:** the customer pays for the robot or its output in ordinary operation.
- **Repeat deployment:** the same customer extends, expands, or reorders after a paid deployment.

Simulation results do not belong here; cite them in the relevant chapter.

**Rules.** Write **Unknown** for any value that is not disclosed, never zero or blank. Supervision means teleoperation, on-site minders, or remote approval, stated as disclosed. Mark vendor-reported figures as vendor-reported in the Source cell.
