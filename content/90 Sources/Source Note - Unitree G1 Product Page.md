## Claims supported

- Power supply "13 string lithium battery"; quick-release battery "9000mAh"; charger "54V 5A". Cited in [[2.2 - Power Distribution and Protection|2.2]] §2.2.6.
- Weight with battery "About 35kg". Cited in [[2.6 - Mechanical Architecture and Structures|2.6]] §2.6.6.
- Cooling System "Local air cooling"; joint motors "Low inertia high-speed internal rotor PMSM (permanent magnet synchronous motor, better response speed and heat dissipation)". Cited in [[2.8 - Thermal Management and Energy Budgets|2.8]] §2.8.6.
- Sensing Sensor "Depth Camera+3D LiDAR"; Joint Encoder "Dual encoder". Cited in [[2.9 - Sensors Calibration and Time Synchronization|2.9]] §2.9.6.
- Basic Computing Power "8-core high-performance CPU"; accessory "High Computing Power Module ... Multiple brands and models available ... such as Orin, etc."; "WiFi 6 、Bluetooth 5.2". Cited in [[2.10 - Embedded Compute and Real-Time Communications|2.10]] §2.10.6.
- Not cited: battery life "About 2h".
