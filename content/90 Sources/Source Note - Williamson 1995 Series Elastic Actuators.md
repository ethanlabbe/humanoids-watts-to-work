## Claims supported

- The actuator "incorporates a series elastic element" and "trades off achievable bandwidth for gains in stable, low noise force control, and protection against shock loads". Cited in [[2.4 - Motors and Actuator Selection|2.4]] §2.4.3.
- Not cited: stiff actuators give "a high bandwidth system, prone to problems of contact instability, noise, and low power density".
