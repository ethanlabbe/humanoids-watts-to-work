- **Title:** Cycloidal Quasi-Direct Drive Actuator Designs with Learning-based Torque Estimation for Legged Robotics
- **Author or organization:** Alvin Zhu, Yusuke Tanaka, Fadi Rafeedi, Dennis Hong; UCLA
- **Publication date:** 2025-03-20 (arXiv v2); accessed 2026-11-22
- **Source type:** Research preprint
- **Stable URL or DOI:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.16591
- **Evidence label:** Author-measured research prototype

## Claims supported

- "a QDD actuator with a 10:1 cycloidal gearbox for legged robots"; continuous and peak torque "evaluated at 37.5 Nm and 89.9 Nm, respectively"; expected peak 120 Nm, lower because of driver current limits; mass 1.40 kg (Table I). Cited in [[2.4 - Motors and Actuator Selection|2.4]] §2.4.4, §2.4.6.
- "The average backlash was calculated at 7.0 arcminutes"; efficiency 82.3%; static back-drive torque 1.99 Nm (Table I). Cited in [[2.4 - Motors and Actuator Selection|2.4]] §2.4.5, and in [[2.5 - Transmissions and Joint Modules|2.5]] §2.5.3, §2.5.6.

## Test conditions

- **Platform or configuration:** One actuator design on the bench; no named robot.
- **Units and measurement boundary:** Output torque at the actuator.
- **Operating conditions:** Cooling conditions for the continuous rating not stated in the part read.
- **Duration, sample size, denominator:** Backlash uncertainty ±1.3 arcmin; other sample sizes not read.

## Limitations

- Preprint. Continuous rating without stated thermal conditions, so 2.4 uses it only as a torque reference.
