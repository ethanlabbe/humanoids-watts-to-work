## Claims supported

- "specifies requirements and tests for the safe operation of portable sealed secondary lithium cells and batteries" containing non-acid electrolyte, "under intended use and reasonably foreseeable misuse". Cited in [[2.1 - Battery Packs Battery Management and Charging|2.1]] §2.1.5, for scope only.
