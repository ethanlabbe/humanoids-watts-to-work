## Claims supported

- "Without a pre-charge circuit, welding can occur within the contactor as it closes and there could be a brief arc resulting in pitting." Cited in [[2.2 - Power Distribution and Protection|2.2]] §2.2.2.
- Not cited: "Failure to manage inrush current can lead to damaged cables, connectors, or fuses"; replacing the mechanical pre-charge contactor with a solid-state switch.
