## Claims supported

All cited in [[1.5 - From Use Case to Engineering Requirements|1.5]].

- Appendix C (§1.5.2): "**Shall** = requirement", "**Will** = facts or declaration of purpose", "**Should** = goal"; requirements "Complete with tolerances for qualitative/performance values"; "Can the system be tested, demonstrated, inspected, or analyzed to show that it satisfies requirements?"; "bidirectionally traceable to higher-level requirements"; "stated in such a manner that it can be uniquely referenced".
- §5.3 (§1.5.7): "Verification is a formal process, using the method of test, analysis, inspection or demonstration, to confirm that a system and its associated hardware and software components satisfy all specified requirements."
- Appendix B (§1.5.7): definitions of Test, Analysis, Inspection, and Demonstration, quoted in the §1.5.7 table.

## Limitations

- Written for NASA programs; the requirement and verification practice is general, but the process weight is heavier than most commercial robot projects use.
- Web edition; section URLs may move.
