## Claims supported

- "25 degrees of freedom (22 fully actuated DoF in the fingers and palm, plus 3 at the wrist)", "quasi-direct-drive tendons" at "low gear ratios of approximately 5:1 to 15:1", "natively force-controlled and fully backdrivable". Cited in [[2.7 - Hands Grippers and End Effectors|2.7]] §2.7.6.
- "IP68 waterproof and food-safe"; "Peak torques reach 3.5 Nm at the thumb CMC and 2.6 Nm at the finger MCP joints, with distal flexion forces up to 45N"; tactile sensing of "normal force, contact location, and shear". Cited in §2.7.6 and [[2.9 - Sensors Calibration and Time Synchronization|2.9]] §2.9.6.
- "hundreds of these hands have already come off a scalable production line"; "capacity to produce 10,000 hands this year". Cited in §2.7.6.
- Motors "live in the forearm", pulling tendons through the wrist. Background for §2.7.2 (tendon drive).

## Test conditions

- **Platform or configuration:** NEO humanoid hand.
- **Units and measurement boundary:** Peak values; continuous ratings not stated.
- **Operating conditions:** Not stated.
- **Duration, sample size, denominator:** "millions of test cycles" for components and finger assemblies; wrist joints "beyond 2 million cycles under high loads"; no failure rates.
