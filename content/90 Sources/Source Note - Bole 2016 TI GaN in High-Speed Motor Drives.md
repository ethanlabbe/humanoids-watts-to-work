## Claims supported

- "With a traditional low-voltage 48V silicon MOSFET-based inverter, the switching losses at 40kHz PWM may already be significantly higher than the conduction losses". Cited in [[2.3 - Motor Drives and Power Electronics|2.3]] §2.3.2.
- GaN transistors "can switch much faster than silicon MOSFETs, significantly reducing switching losses". Background for [[2.3 - Motor Drives and Power Electronics|2.3]] §2.3.3, not quoted.
- Not cited: reducing switching loss by downsizing MOSFETs "leads to increased conduction losses" (the chapter states the trade-off in its own words); reference-design losses of 4.95 W at 40 kHz and 5.65 W at 100 kHz.
