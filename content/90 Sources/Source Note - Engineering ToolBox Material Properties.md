## Claims supported

- Young's modulus (GPa): Structural steel ASTM-A36 200; Aluminum Alloys 70; Magnesium metal 45; Titanium Alloy 105 to 120; Nylon-6 2 to 4; ABS plastics 1.4 to 3.1; Carbon Fiber Reinforced Plastic 150. Cited in [[2.6 - Mechanical Architecture and Structures|2.6]] §2.6.2, §2.6.4.
- Density (10³ kg/m³): Steel 7.82; Aluminum Alloys 2.6 to 2.85; Magnesium 1.74; Titanium 4.5; Nylon 6 1.12 to 1.17; ABS 1.06. Cited in §2.6.2.

## Limitations

- Typical values only; a design uses the supplier's data for the exact grade.
- The titanium density is for the pure metal, used with the alloy modulus; CFRP modulus is along the fibres and no CFRP density is given.
