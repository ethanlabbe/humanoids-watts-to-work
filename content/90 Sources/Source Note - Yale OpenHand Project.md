## Claims supported

- "Underactuated hands have been shown to improve the generality of simple grippers by adaptively conforming to the surface of objects without the explicit need for sensors or complicated feedback systems." Cited in [[2.7 - Hands Grippers and End Effectors|2.7]] §2.7.2.
- The released designs "feature tendon-driven underactuated fingers", made by 3D printing combined with resin casting. Cited in §2.7.6.
