## Claims supported

- Covers UN Nos. 3090, 3091, 3480, and 3481. "All cell types shall be subjected to tests T.1 to T.6 and T.8." Rechargeable batteries: T.1 to T.5 and T.7; non-rechargeable batteries: T.1 to T.5. Cited in [[2.1 - Battery Packs Battery Management and Charging|2.1]] §2.1.5.
