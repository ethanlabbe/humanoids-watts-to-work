## Claims supported

- "Time-Sensitive Networking (TSN) is a set of standards specified by IEEE 802"; the task group's charter is "to provide deterministic connectivity through IEEE 802 networks, i.e., guaranteed packet transport with bounded latency, low packet delay variation, and low packet loss". Cited in [[2.10 - Embedded Compute and Real-Time Communications|2.10]] §2.10.2.
- Not cited: profiles for industrial automation (IEC/IEEE 60802), automotive in-vehicle and aerospace onboard Ethernet.
