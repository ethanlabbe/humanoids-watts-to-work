## Claims supported

- "After this reduction in grasp types, the final number of valid grasp types is 33"; "they can be reduced to a set of 17 more prototypical grasps". Cited in [[2.7 - Hands Grippers and End Effectors|2.7]] §2.7.2.
- Power, intermediate and precision grasps: in the power grip "all movements of the object have to be evoked by the arm"; in precision handling "the hand is able to perform intrinsic movements on the object without having to move the arm"; the intermediate class has "elements of power and precision grasps". Cited in §2.7.2.
- Three opposition types: pad, palm and side opposition. Cited in §2.7.2.
