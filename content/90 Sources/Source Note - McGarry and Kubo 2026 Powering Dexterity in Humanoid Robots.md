## Claims supported

- Two "emerging" distribution architectures: a battery-derived 48 V rail as a non-isolated bus, or an isolated, regulated bus that "simplifies the overall safety strategy". Cited in [[2.2 - Power Distribution and Protection|2.2]] §2.2.6.
- A non-isolated bus "often exceeds Safety Extra Low Voltage (SELV) safety limits". No threshold or standard is given. Cited in [[2.2 - Power Distribution and Protection|2.2]] §2.2.3.
- A shoulder "would typically draw around 500W continuous with up to 1200W peak for short durations". Cited in [[2.2 - Power Distribution and Protection|2.2]] §2.2.4.
- Hand and wrist motors "supplied from a lower input voltage, 12V or 24V". Cited in [[2.2 - Power Distribution and Protection|2.2]] §2.2.6.
