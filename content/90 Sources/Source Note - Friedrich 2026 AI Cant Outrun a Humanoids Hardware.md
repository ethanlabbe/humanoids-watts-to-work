## Claims supported

- Humanoids "are moving toward higher voltages, specifically 48V DC architectures". Cited in [[2.2 - Power Distribution and Protection|2.2]] §2.2.6.
- "resistive losses scale with the square of current, reducing current by four reduces wiring losses by a factor of 16" (12 V to 48 V). Cited in [[2.2 - Power Distribution and Protection|2.2]] §2.2.2.
- Not cited: during rapid deceleration motors can produce "transient voltages considerably higher than the nominal DC bus voltage" (the chapter uses Invertek for regeneration).
