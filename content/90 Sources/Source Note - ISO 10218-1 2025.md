## Claims supported

- The standard specifies requirements for inherently safe design, risk reduction measures, and information for use of industrial robots, treating the robot as partly completed machinery. Integration and robot applications are covered in ISO 10218-2:2025. Formerly cited in 1.4 §1.4.5 for scope; removed (2026-11-22 redraft of 1.4). Held for Chapter 5.3 (Planned).
- Also for Chapter 5.3 (Planned): per publisher summaries, the 2025 edition adds mode requirements and cybersecurity requirements as they apply to safety, and absorbs collaborative-application content formerly in ISO/TS 15066.
