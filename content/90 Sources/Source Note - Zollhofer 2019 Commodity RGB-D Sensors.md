## Claims supported

- Stereo: fails on uniformly coloured walls and in low light; depth quality "degrades with increasing distance to the scene and improves for larger baselines"; "multiple devices do not interfere with each other"; cheap and "quite energy efficient". Cited in [[2.9 - Sensors Calibration and Time Synchronization|2.9]] §2.9.3.
- Structured light: does "not work under strong sunlight"; the pattern "might also be absorbed by dark objects, reflected by specular objects, or refracted by transparent objects"; working range "normally up to 15 meters"; interference between multiple sensors. Cited in §2.9.3.
- Time-of-flight: "flying pixels" at depth discontinuities; "multi-path interference" from mirrors or glass; dark materials leave holes; "struggles under strong sunlight"; motion blur. Cited in §2.9.3.
