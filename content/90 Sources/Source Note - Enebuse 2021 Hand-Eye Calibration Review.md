## Claims supported

- Camera "in a fixed position with a fixed field of view (eye-to-hand configuration)" or "mounted on the hand of the robot (eye-in-hand configuration)". Cited in [[2.9 - Sensors Calibration and Time Synchronization|2.9]] §2.9.2.
- The most common formulation "requires finding a solution to the homogeneous transform equation AX = XB". Cited in §2.9.2.
- Checkerboard is "the most common calibration pattern"; circle grids also widely used. Cited in §2.9.2.
- Offline calibration does not suit applications where "frequent changes in setup and recalibration is an expensive operation". Cited in §2.9.3.
- "an accuracy level of 1 mm may be required for spot welding operation in the automotive industry". Cited in §2.9.4.
