## Claims supported

- A fall predictor plus a protective policy that "remains dormant until the predictor identifies a fall as unavoidable". Cited in [[2.6 - Mechanical Architecture and Structures|2.6]] §2.6.3.
- On a full-scale Unitree G1 it "reduced peak contact forces by 68.3%, peak joint torques by 78.4%, and eliminated 99.3% of collisions with vulnerable components". Cited in §2.6.3, §2.6.6.

- **Platform or configuration:** Unitree G1.
