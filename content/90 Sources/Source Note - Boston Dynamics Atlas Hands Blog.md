## Claims supported

- "In practice, hand design is a ruthless tradeoff. Dexterity, strength, ruggedness, cost, repairability, and sensing all compete with each other." Cited in [[2.7 - Hands Grippers and End Effectors|2.7]] §2.7.3.
- "A truly general purpose hand is not really an achievable goal". Cited in §2.7.3, §2.7.7.
- New hand: "four fingers, including a more dexterous thumb, with a total of 13 DOFs, maintaining highly transparent direct actuation at the joints, all with a single actuator type"; "dense pressure tactile sensors that cover the fingertips and palm"; previous hand had seven DoF. Cited in §2.7.6.
- No pinky: "an extra finger means three more actuators, with the corresponding extra cost, volume, and probability of something breaking". Cited in §2.7.3, §2.7.6.
