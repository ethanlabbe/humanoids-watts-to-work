## Claims supported

- "The first numeral refers to the protection against solid objects and is rated on a scale from 0 (no protection) to 6 (no ingress of dust)"; the second rates protection against liquids "from 0 (no protection) to 9". Cited in [[2.6 - Mechanical Architecture and Structures|2.6]] §2.6.2.
- IEC 60529 "Applies to the classification of degrees of protection provided by enclosures for electrical equipment with a rated voltage not exceeding 72,5 kV". Background for §2.6.2 and the ingress test in §2.6.5.
