## Claims supported

- "a proprioceptive actuation paradigm"; "Proprioceptive actuation uses collocated force control at the joints". Cited in [[2.4 - Motors and Actuator Selection|2.4]] §2.4.3.
- "a unique combination of high torque density, high-bandwidth force control, and the ability to mitigate impacts through backdrivability"; the "impact mitigation factor" (IMF) "to quantify backdrivability at impact". Cited in [[2.4 - Motors and Actuator Selection|2.4]] §2.4.3. Also cited in [[2.5 - Transmissions and Joint Modules|2.5]] §2.5.2 for reflected inertia.
