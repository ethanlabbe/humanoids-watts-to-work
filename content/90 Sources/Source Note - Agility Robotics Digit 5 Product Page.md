## Claims supported

- Digit 5 described as "Our first cooperatively safe humanoid", with payload "23kg/50lbs", runtime "20HRS/24HRS" (work/shift), a "10:1" run-to-charge ratio, and a "swappable gripper design". Cited in [[1.2 - What Is a Humanoid Robot|1.2]] §1.2.4 and [[2.7 - Hands Grippers and End Effectors|2.7]] §2.7.6 (swappable gripper only).
- "Now with a 10:1 run-to-charge ratio, Digit will spend less time at the dock". Cited in [[2.1 - Battery Packs Battery Management and Charging|2.1]] §2.1.6, with the page's footnote that specifications are "preliminary estimates based on internal testing".
- "Certain safety features are still in development." Cited in [[1.3 - Why Build a Humanoid|1.3]] §1.3.2 and §1.3.5, and [[1.4 - How a Humanoid Works - The Full System Map|1.4]] §1.4.5.
